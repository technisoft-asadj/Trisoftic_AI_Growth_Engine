How to delete your data

To remove the data TriSoftic AI Growth Engine holds about you or your Facebook Page:

  1. In Facebook, open Settings → Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites), find TriSoftic AI Growth Engine and remove it. This stops the app from accessing your Page.
  2. Email [CONTACT EMAIL - replace before publishing] with the name of your Page and ask us to delete your data. We will delete the stored Page connection, access token, content and performance figures and confirm by reply within 30 days.
