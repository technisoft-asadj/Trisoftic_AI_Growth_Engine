Privacy Policy

TriSoftic AI Growth Engine ("the app", "we"). Last updated: 18 November 2026.

The app helps a business plan, write, approve, schedule and publish marketing content to its own social media Pages, and measure how that content performed. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Who uses the app

The app is used by the business that operates it and by its staff, who sign in with their own accounts. It is not a consumer app and it does not collect information from the general public who see the published posts.

What we collect

  • Account details of staff who sign in: name, work email, and role.
  • Facebook Page connection: when an authorised person connects a Facebook Page, we receive the Page's name and ID and an access token that lets the app post to that Page. The token is stored encrypted.
  • Content that staff write or approve: post text, images and videos, schedules and approval records.
  • Performance figures for the Page's own posts, such as reactions, comments, shares, views and reach, as counts. We use them to show results and to plan the next round of content.

We do not collect the names, profiles or messages of the people who react to or comment on posts, and we do not read private messages.

How we use it

  • To publish content that the business has approved to its connected Page, at the time it chose.
  • To show staff how their posts performed.
  • To help write drafts and plans. Text the business provides about its products may be sent to an AI service to generate suggestions.

Facebook permissions

The app asks only for the permissions it needs: to list the Pages you manage, to publish posts to a Page, and to read that Page's own posts and insights. We use Facebook data only to provide these features. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it for any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app (hosting, and the AI service used to draft content), and where the law requires it.

Retention and deletion

We keep data while the business uses the app. Disconnecting a Facebook Page removes its stored access token. You can ask us to delete your data at any time; see how to request deletion. You can also remove the app from your Facebook account under Settings → Business Integrations.

Security

Access tokens are stored encrypted, access is limited to signed-in staff, and publishing requires an approval step by a person.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 16.
